To remove a battery, follow these steps:

1.  Remove the left rear cover—see {% tagref "access_internal_parts" /%}.
2.  Open the top compartment.
3.  Open the left front hatch.
4.  Lift off the left front hatch from the hinges.

    {% image id="dc713c61-fbdc-45e7-81f2-c3154672acac" alt="Front compartment hatch removed" /%}

5.  Disconnect the battery.
6.  Remove the battery locking clip. Use a T20 bit.

    {% image id="3cc93337-548d-4d44-ae7f-85a9301a1c2d" alt="Battery locking clip being removed" /%}

7.  Pull out the battery while supporting it underneath with the other hand.

    {% image id="dc3bdbe5-f19a-4466-a544-d4afdf66c949" alt="The battery being pulled out of the robot" /%}
