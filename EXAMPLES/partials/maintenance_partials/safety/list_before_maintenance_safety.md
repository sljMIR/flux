Before performing any maintenance, follow these safety precautions:

* Always wear safety shoes while performing maintenance on MiR robots.

* Only use equipment that you are trained to use. If the instructions call for equipment that you are not familiar with, request help or training from other personnel.

* Always electrically ground yourself before performing maintenance that involves interaction with any internal parts.

* Verify that the lifting capability of lifting equipment can safely support the robot application's weight:
  * Lifting equipment must be able to support at least {% $min_lift_weight %}.

  * Verify that slings, chains, or similar that are used to lift the robot are equally rated to support the robot and top module's weight.

  * Verify that eye-bolts used for lifting the robot are the correct size for the mounting holes in the top plate, and that they are in good order—see [Lifting the robot](../preventative_maintenance_tasks/How to lift the robot.htm).
