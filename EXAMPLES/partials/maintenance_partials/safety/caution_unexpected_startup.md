{% notice type="caution" %}

If the robot starts up unexpectedly, there is a risk of injury to nearby personnel or damage to equipment.

  * Always deactivate robots from active fleets or disconnect them from the network before performing any maintenance to prevent accidental movements, fleet commands, or manual control commands during maintenance procedures.

  * Always physically disconnect the internal battery of the robot before performing any maintenance or parts replacement. Some maintenance procedures will require power to verify the correct function of certain functions and features. These procedures clearly state when to connect the battery.
  
{% /notice %}
