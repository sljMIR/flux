The robot is not classified as WEEE and can be disposed of in accordance with the applicable local regulations (see European directive 2012/19/EU, Article 2, paragraph 4) d) and 4) e)).

Legal obligations apply to the robot's batteries. Batteries must be recycled. Contact your distributor to get specific information about their take back service.
