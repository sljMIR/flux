# Check the hydraulic hoses {% tag "check_the_hydraulic_hoses" /%}

<image: "check_hydraulic_hose.png">

## Purpose

To ensure that the integrity of the hydraulics system is not compromised and to discover any potential leakage points in time to do the necessary preventative maintenance.

{% partial file="products/_maintenance_partials/frequency/text_yearly.md" /%}

## Procedure

1.  Visually inspect the hoses going between the hydraulic pump unit and the hydraulic cylinder. Look for any signs of cracking or defects in the rubber tubing. Feel along the tubing with your hands to feel for any rough spots or signs of degradation.
    
2.  Look for signs of oil leakage at both connection points of the hydraulic hoses. Use a paper towel to wipe connection points to test for any liquids. The oil is see-through and can be hard to see in dimly lit areas.

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

There are no signs of cracks or defects in any hydraulic hoses.
