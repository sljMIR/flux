# Check the forks and fork console {% tag "check_the_forks_and_fork_console" /%}

<image: "forks_maintenance.png">

## Purpose

To ensure that the strength and durability of the forks and the robot's lift capability is not compromised.

{% partial file="products/_maintenance_partials/frequency/text_yearly.md" /%}

To complete this task, the robot does not need to be powered.

Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery.

*   When performing maintenance on robots, you risk electrical shock from electrical wires and connectors if internal components are exposed, crushing, collision, or pinching from unexpected movements.

## Procedure

1.  Visually inspect the entire fork and fork console assembly from all angles. Look for any defects, cracks or signs of deformation.
    
2.  Visually inspect all welding points for cracks or damages.

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

*   The fork and fork console assembly have no visual cracks or defects, and are in good working order.
