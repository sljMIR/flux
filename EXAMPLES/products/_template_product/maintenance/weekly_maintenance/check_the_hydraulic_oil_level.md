# Check the hydraulic oil level {% tag "check_the_hydraulic_oil_level" /%}

<image: "hydraulic_oil_level.png">

## Purpose

To ensure that the hydraulic oil level is not changing, suggesting that the hydraulic oil is leaking from the system somewhere.

{% partial file="products/_maintenance_partials/frequency/text_weekly.md" /%}

To complete this task, the robot does not need to be powered after returning the forks to the lowest position.

Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery.

*   When performing maintenance on robots, you risk electrical shock from electrical wires and connectors if internal components are exposed, crushing, collision, or pinching from unexpected movements.

## Procedure

1.  Put the forks in the lowest position so that all hydraulic oil is returned to the reservoir.
    
2.  Access the front compartment. See [Accessing the internal parts of MiR1200 Pallet Jack](../general topics/accessing_internals_1200.htm).
    
3.  Observe the level in the reservoir. It should always be above the minimum marking when the forks are in their lowest position.
    
    Pay attention to any signs of changes in level over time. If you suspect that the level is changing, thoroughly inspect the hydraulic hoses, as well as any possible leakage points around the hydraulic cylinder, pump unit, and drip tray.

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

*   The hydraulic oil level is within tolerance.
