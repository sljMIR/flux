# Check the drip tray {% tag="check_the_drip_tray" %}

<image: "drip_tray.png">

## Purpose

To ensure that no leakage of hydraulic oil is present under the hydraulic pump and reservoir combo unit.

{% partial file="products/_maintenance_partials/frequency/text_weekly.md" /%}

To complete this task, the robot does not need to be powered.

Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery.

*   When performing maintenance on robots, you risk electrical shock from electrical wires and connectors if internal components are exposed, crushing, collision, or pinching from unexpected movements.

## Procedure

1.  Access the front compartment. See [Accessing the internal parts of MiR1200 Pallet Jack](../general topics/accessing_internals_1200.htm).
    
2.  Visually inspect that the drip tray is dry and that there are no signs of leakages. You can use a flashlight to better observe liquids.
    
3.  Visually inspect that the area around the bottom of the hydraulic cylinder is dry and that there are no signs of leakages there either.

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

*   The drip tray is dry, and there are no signs of leakages.
    
*   The area around the bottom of the hydraulic cylinder is dry, and there are no signs of leakages.
