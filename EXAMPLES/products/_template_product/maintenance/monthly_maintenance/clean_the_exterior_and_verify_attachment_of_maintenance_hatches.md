# Clean the  exterior and verify attachment of maintenance hatches {% tag "clean_the_exterior_and_verify_attachment_of_maintenance_hatches" /%}

<image: "mir600_1350_maintain_covers.png">

## Purpose

To limit the amount of dust particles and other debris that can enter the internal parts of the robot. You should also inspect the   general condition of the maintenance hatches.

{% partial file="products/_maintenance_partials/frequency/text_monthly.md" /%}

{% partial file="products/_maintenance_partials/safety/warning_turn_off_robot.md" /%}


## Procedure

1.  Inspect and verify that all maintenance hatches are attached correctly.
    
2.  Inspect and verify that there are no damages to the maintenance hatches. Open and close the maintenance hatches to verify condition of hinges and mounting.
    
3.  Wipe down the maintenance hatches with a damp cloth. Pay particular attention to the horizontal surfaces of the maintenance hatches, as most dust and debris will collect there.

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

*   All maintenance hatches are attached correctly.
    
*   No damages to any maintenance hatches.
    
*   Dust and debris is removed from the maintenance hatches.
