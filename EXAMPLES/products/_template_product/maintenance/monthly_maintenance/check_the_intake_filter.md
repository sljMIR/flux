# Check the intake filter {% tag="check_the_intake_filter" %}

<image: "air_filter_location.png">

## Purpose

To ensure that the robot can intake sufficient air to cool the internal components. If the filter is dirty or blocked, it can severely inhibit the robot's performance and may lead to spontaneous shutdowns because of insufficient cooling.

{% partial file="products/_maintenance_partials/frequency/text_monthly.md" /%}

To complete this task, the robot does not need to be powered.

Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery.

*   When performing maintenance on robots, you risk electrical shock from electrical wires and connectors if internal components are exposed, crushing, collision, or pinching from unexpected movements.

## Procedure

1.  Access the front compartment. See [Accessing the internal parts of MiR1200 Pallet Jack](../general topics/accessing_internals_1200.htm).
    
2.  Remove the intake filter box. Depending on your robot revision, you may have either a Torx screw (T20), a quarter-turn fastner, or a finger screw.
    
3.  Inspect the filter—replace if dirty. It is not recommended to clean and reuse the old filter. New filters can be ordered from MiR with the order number 450784.
    
    <image: "remove_filter_1200.png">

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

The intake filter is clean and unobstructed.
