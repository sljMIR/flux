# Inspect and clean the drive wheel {% tag "inspect_and_clean_the_drive_wheel" /%}

<image: "drive_wheel_location.png">

## Purpose

To ensure that nothing is entangled in the drive wheels, that any dirt and debris is removed, and that nothing causes irregular driving.

{% partial file="products/_maintenance_partials/frequency/text_monthly.md" /%}

To complete this task, the robot does not need to be powered.

Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery.

*   When performing maintenance on robots, you risk electrical shock from electrical wires and connectors if internal components are exposed, crushing, collision, or pinching from unexpected movements.

## Procedure

1.  Access the bottom compartment and remove the foot guard. See [Accessing the internal parts of MiR1200 Pallet Jack](../general topics/accessing_internals_1200.htm).
    
2.  Clean the drive wheel using a damp cloth. Inspect the surface for any imbedded debris.
    
    <image: "drive_wheel_diameter.png">

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

*   The drive wheel is clean and there is no imbedded debris in the wheel surface.
