# Inspect and clean the support leg rollers {% tag "inspect_and_clean_the_support_leg_rollers" /%}

<image: "support_leg_rollers.png">

## Purpose

To ensure that the support leg rollers have no debris or foreign objects stuck in them, and that they rotate smoothly and evenly as intended.

## Procedure

1.  Move the forks all the way up and secure them in place with M12 safety bolts.
    
    <image: "safety_bolts.png">
    
2.  Turn off the robot and disconnect the battery.
    
3.  Tilt up the support legs in order to turn the support leg roller by hand.
    
4.  Clean the support leg rollers with a damp cloth.
    
5.  Visually inspect and feel for foreign objects in the roller surface or bearings.
    
6.  Listen for grinding noises when turning the support leg rollers by hand.

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

*   The support leg rollers are clean and have no debris or foreign objects stuck in them or the bearings.
    
*   The support leg rollers rotate smoothly and evenly when rotated.
    
*   There are no grinding noises from the bearings when the support leg roller is rotated.
