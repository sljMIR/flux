# Lubricate the chain {% tag "lubricate_the_chain" /%}

<image: "chain_maintenance.png">

## Purpose

To ensure that the chain performs optimally and to extend the longevity of the chain. The chain must be lubricated for every 250 hydraulic operation hours. Always use Rex® RexOil high performance chain spray or similar product for lubricating the chain.

*   If using the robot in a 24-hour/7-day operation, quarterly lubrication is recommended.

*   If using the robot in an 8-hour/5-day operation, half-yearly lubrication is recommended.

## Procedure

1.  Unscrew the eight screws (six shown in image) holding the polycarbonate glass face in place. Use a T30 bit. When reassembling, tighten these screws to 6 Nm.
    
    <image: "polycarbonate_glass_screws.png">
    
2.  Remove the display from the polycarbonate glass face. Use a T25 bit. When reassembling, tighten these screws to 0.75 Nm.
    
    <image: "unscrew_hmi.png">
    
3.  The polycarbonate glass face can now be removed. Carefully set it aside. The display can be placed on the top cover while working on the robot.
    
4.  Wipe the chain thoroughly with a clean, lint-free cloth to remove dirt, dust and other contaminants from all exposed surfaces.
    
5.  Ensure that the chain is dry and then apply Rex RexOil high performance chain spray or similar product to all joints of the chain at a distance that promotes penetration into internal parts of the joints, while still having some distance.  

    It is recommended to hold a piece of cardboard or other disposable material behind the chain while applying the oil to avoid overspray on other parts of the robot.
    
6.  Move the forks all the way up and down 10 times to distribute the lubricant evenly.
    
7.  Wipe the chain using a clean, lint-free cloth to remove excess lubricant from the outer surfaces. Lubrication is intended for the chain joints. Any residual film on external surfaces will attract dirt and may accelerate wear.

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

*   The chain is sufficiently lubricated.
