# Check mast rollers {% tag="check_mast_rollers" %}

<image: "mast_roller_maintenance.png">

## Purpose

To ensure that the mast rollers are in good working order, that none of the rollers are blocked, moving poorly or exhibit signs of excessive wear.

{% partial file="products/_maintenance_partials/frequency/text_half-yearly.md" /%}

To complete this task, the battery must be connected, and the robot must be turned on.

When performing maintenance, you risk electrical shock from exposed electrical wires, and crushing, collision, or pinching from unexpected movement.

*   Be careful of exposed electrical wires.
    
*   Avoid inserting your hands between actuators or other moving parts of the robot.

## Procedure

1.  Visually inspect the mast rollers and the inner rails of the mast. Look for any signs that a mast roller may be blocked. Look for signs of friction/excessive traction wear in the inner rails.
    
2.  Move the forks to the maximum height.
    
3.  Slowly move the forks to the bottom position. Listen for any grinding or squeaking noise from the mast rollers.

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

*   The mast rollers exhibit no grinding or squeaking noise when moving.
    
*   There are no signs of blockages or friction within the inner rails of the mast.
