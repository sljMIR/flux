# Lubricate the mast rails {% tag "lubricate_the_mast_rails" /%}

<image: "mast_rails.png">

## Purpose

To ensure that the mast insides are protected against rust and the rollers can move freely and effortlessly up and down the inner rails of the mast. As a basis, the mast rails should be lubricated for every 250 hydraulic operation hours. Always use Anderol® PQAA-2 PLUS or similar product for lubricating the mast rails.

*   If using the robot in a 24-hour/7-day operation, quarterly lubrication is recommended.

*   If using the robot in an 8-hour/5-day operation, half-yearly lubrication is recommended.

To complete this task, the robot does not need to be powered.

Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery.

*   When performing maintenance on robots, you risk electrical shock from electrical wires and connectors if internal components are exposed, crushing, collision, or pinching from unexpected movements.

## Procedure

1.  Move the forks to the bottom position and turn off the robot.
    
2.  Clean the inside of the mast rails above the fork console to remove old grease, grime and debris.
    
3.  Relubricate the inside of the mast rails above the fork console.
    
4.  Turn on the robot and move the forks to the top position.
    
5.  Insert a set of M12x1.75 100 mm safety bolts to ensure that the forks cannot move, and turn off the robot with the forks in this position.
    
6.  Clean the inside of the mast rails in the accessible areas below the fork console to remove old grease, grime and debris.
    
7.  Relubricate the inside of the mast rails below the fork console.
    
8.  Remove the safety bolts and turn on the robot.
    
9.  Move the forks up and down the entire span 10 times. Leave them at the top position and turn off the robot.
    
10.  Wipe off any excess grease that has been pushed outside the mast profile or has gathered in larger chunks.

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

*   There is a thin film of lubrication on the entire inside of the mast rail.
    
*   The forks move up and down in a uniform and smooth manner.
