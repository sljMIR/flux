# Lubricate the drive wheel bearings {% tag "lubricate_the_drive_wheel_bearings" /%}

<image: "mir600_1350_grease_nip.png">

## Purpose

To ensure maximum longevity of the drive wheel bearings and frictionless operation of the drive wheel.

{% partial file="products/_maintenance_partials/frequency/text_half-yearly.md" /%}

To complete this task, the robot does not need to be powered.

Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery.

*   When performing maintenance on robots, you risk electrical shock from electrical wires and connectors if internal components are exposed, crushing, collision, or pinching from unexpected movements.

## Procedure

Follow the procedure for lubricating the drive wheel bearings as outlined in How to lubricate the drive wheel bearings on MiR600 and MiR1350. You can find this guide on MiR Support Portal.

See a [video](https://vimeo.com/931073708) of the process on the Mobile Industrial Robots profile on vimeo.com.

## Pass criteria <image: "mmg_pass.svg">

*   Both drive wheel bearings have been lubricated using the specified lubricant.
