## Lift the robot

To lift the robot:

* Use four M12 eye-bolts in each corner of the robot (not included).

* Make sure the thread engagement is at least 30 mm when a top plate is mounted to the robot.

* All slings must be of equal length and have a lift rating of at least 1 000 kg.

* The angle between the top cover and sling must be greater than 60°.

* The bolts must be parallel with the diagonal between opposite corners.
