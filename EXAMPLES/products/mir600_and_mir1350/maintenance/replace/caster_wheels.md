Determine relevant and necessary PPE based on the task, environment, and experience of personnel. The following is a list of common PPE you must consider:

  * Safety shoes to protect from impact or puncture hazards.

  * Safety glasses and face shield to protect from flying debris or chemical agents.

  * Hard hat to protect from falling or flying objects.

  * Gloves to protect against sharp objects, electrical hazards, chemical agents, and extreme temperatures.

Electrical components can be damaged by electrostatic discharge. Consider moving the robot to an ESD protected area and using ESD protected clothing and tools.

This guide explains how to replace the four caster wheels on a MiR250 robot. When you replace the caster wheels, you must replace all four wheels at the same time to make sure the robot is level.

## Things you will need

  * A 65 Nm torque wrench with the following sockets:

    * 13 mm hex

    * 19 mm hex

  * 2 × 13 mm wrench

## Kit contents

The kit contains the following items:

  * 4 × caster wheels with forks

  * 4 × M12 lock nuts

  * 4 × 13 mm M8 lock nuts

  * 4 × M8 bolts

## Before replacing the caster wheel

  1. Remove the rear cover by pressing the two white buttons and pulling off the cover. Loosen the bottom corners one at the time by pulling out each corner.

<image: "rear\_cover\_buttons\_2.png"> <image: "front\_bottom\_corners.png">

  2.      1. Disconnect the battery (MiR250 hardware version 1.0).

        1. Turn the Battery lock pin clockwise to unlock the battery connector.

<image: "unlock\_battery\_connected.png">

        2. Pull the Battery lever downwards to disconnect the battery.

<image: "disconnect\_battery.png">

        3. Turn the Battery lock pin counterclockwise to lock the battery connector.

<image: "lock\_battery\_disconnected.png">

     2. Disconnect the battery (MiR250 hardware version 2.0 and higher)

        1. Pull out the Battery lock pin while pushing the Battery lever downwards.

<image: "250\_mkII\_disconnect\_battery.png">

        2. The Battery lock pin will click into place and lock the battery connector once the Battery lever has been pushed all the way down.

<image: "250\_mkII\_battery\_disconnected\_lock\_battery\_pin.png">

  3. Raise the robot from the ground using a lifting device so either or both bogies are raised from the ground and not supporting the robot. Place the robot on wooden blocks or similar. See the manual for your application for instructions on lifting the robot.

Placing the robot so it is resting on its charging plates can damage the plates.

  * Make sure the charging plates beneath the robot are not supporting the robot's weight.

<image: "mir250\_wooden\_blocks\_front.png">

## Replacing the caster wheel

  1. Remove the M8 lock nuts on the caster wheels and remove the caster wheels from the brackets. Use a 13 mm hex socket.

<image: "250\_unscrew\_caster\_wheels.png">

  2. Remove the M12 lock nuts holding the caster wheel brackets in place. Use a 19 mm hex socket. Remove the brackets from the robot. Discard the used lock nuts.

<image: "mir250\_unscrew\_caster\_wheel\_bracket.png">

<image: "mir250\_remove\_caster\_wheel\_bracket.png">

  3. Fasten the new caster wheel brackets to the robot with the supplied M12 lock nuts. Use a 19 mm socket. The caster wheel brackts must be tightened to 65 Nm.

  4. Fasten the new caster wheels to the new brackets on the robot with the supplied M8 lock nuts and bolts. Use a 13 mm socket. Tighten the nuts until the black center part (hub) cannot rotate, but the gray part (wheel track) can rotate freely.
