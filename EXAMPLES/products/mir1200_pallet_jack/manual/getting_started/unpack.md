## Remove the sides of the box

1. Place the box with the robot so that there is at least seven meters of free space at the front of the box. The front of the box is where the center of gravity mark is located.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/enhanced_front.webp" alt="Front of the box showing center of gravity mark" /%}

    {% notice type="caution" %}

    The center of gravity is at the front. Lifting the pallet from the back risks tipping the box over.

    * Always pick up the pallet with a forklift from the front of the box.

    {% /notice %}

2. Check that the TiltWatch sticker and the ShockWatch sticker.

     If the TiltWatch shows more than 30° tilt or if the ShockWatch indicator has been activated, make a note on the bill of delivery receipt, and inspect the robot for damage. If damage is discovered, request immediate inspection from the carrier.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/tilt_label.webp" alt="TiltWatch and ShockWatch stickers showing the robot has been safely shipped." /%}

3. Remove the clamp removal tool lodged behind a clamp in one of the top corners..

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/clamp_tool.webp" alt="Clamp tool location in one of the top corners." /%}

4. Remove all of the clamps around the lid of the box.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/lid_clamps.webp" alt="The clamps around the lid being removed." /%}

    Use the clamp removal tool by lodging it into the clamps where there are small sills. Use a hammer if necessary. Turn the tool to the side to release the clamps.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/clamp_sill.webp" alt="The clamp tool being lodged into a clamp with the help of a hammer and the clamp being removed." /%}

5. Slide the lid to the side to a person ready to receive it on the ground.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/lid_off.webp" alt="Removing the box lid" /%}

6. Remove one of the long sides of the box. Hold the side in place while removing the clamps.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/side_off.webp" alt="Removing side of the box" /%}

7.  Remove the two ends, and then the remaining side.

## Prepare the ramp

1. Remove the bag of ramp screws from the ramp fastening.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/screw_bag.webp" alt="The bag og screws fastened to the ramp fastening."  /%}

2. Remove the ramp fastening.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/ramp_fastening.webp" alt="A man removing the ramp fastening." /%}

3.  Lay the three ramp-pieces out in front of the pallet.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/ramp_building1.webp" alt="Laying out ramp pieces" /%}

4.  Use the supplied wing screws to fasten the ramp pieces to the pallet and to each other. Do not screw the wing screws so far into the ramp pieces that they stick out on the other side. This might damage the robot while it drives down the ramp.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/ramp_building2.webp" alt="Fastening ramp pieces" /%}

## Remove protection and additional items

1. Unscrew the two screws holding the Halo protection in place, and remove the protection. Use a socket wrench with a 24 mm hex socket.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/halo_protection.webp" alt="Location of the two screws that need to removed to remove the Halo protection." /%}

2. Remove the package with the printed documents and accessories.
3. Unscrew the eight screws on the blocks at the back, in front of, and to the sides of the robot. Use a socket wrench with a 13 mm hex socket. Remove the blocks.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/side_blocks.webp" alt="Removing side blocks" /%}

4. Unscrew the four nuts under the fork fastening. Use a socket wrench with a 17 mm hex socket. Remove the fastening.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/fork_fastening.webp" alt="Fork fastening removal" /%}

5. Open the two front hatches—see {% tagref "access_internal_parts" /%}.
6. Remove the support foam.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/support_foam.webp" alt="Support foam removal" /%}

7. Open the top hatch—see {% tagref "access_internal_parts" /%}.
8. Unscrew the two screws holding the electronics box shipping brace. Use a 6 mm Hex socket.

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/support_bracket.webp" alt="Electronics box shipping brace" /%}

9. Pull up the electronics box shipping brace and remove it.

## Drive the robot off the ramp

1. Close the top hatch and the two front hatches.
2. Connect the batteries—see {% tagref "connect_batteries" /%}
3. Connect the main battery connector—see {% tagref "power_connection" /%}.
4. Turn on the robot—see {% tagref "turn_on" /%}.
5. Drive the robot off the ramp using the tiller—see {% tagref "operate_manually" /%}

    {% image src="/images/mir1200_pallet_jack/packaging/down_the_ramp.webp" alt="Robot driving down the ramp" /%}
