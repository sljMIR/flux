## Top compartment

The top compartment holds no components. The Emergency stop buttons are placed on top of the compartment cover—see {% tagref tag="emergency_stop" /%}).

## Halo

The Halo compartment contains the following components:

* Speaker
* Wi-Fi antennas
* 3D LiDAR
* Blue light
* Signal lights, 2 pcs
* Safety buzzer
