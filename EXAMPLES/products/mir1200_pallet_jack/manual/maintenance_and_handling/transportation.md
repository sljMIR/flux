## Lift the robot

To lift the robot:

*   Mount the two supplied M16 eye-bolts into the mounting holes on top of the Halo on the robot.
*   Ensure that the eye-bolts are screwed in all the way.
*   Ensure both slings are of equal length and have a lift rating of at least 2 000 kg.
*   Ensure that the slings do not come into contact with the 3D LiDAR.
