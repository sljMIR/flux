## Things you will need

  * A T30 screwdriver

  * A 0.3 Nm torque wrench with a 5.5 mm socket

## Kit contents

450318: 1 × short status light LED band (for the front and rear)

450319: 1 × long status light LED band (for the left and right sides)

## Before replacing the status light LED bands

  1. Push the two buttons on the rear cover, and remove the cover from the robot.

<image: "rear\_press\_buttons.png"> <image: "rear\_remove.png">

  2. Disconnect the battery.

     * Hardware 1.0 only

Turn the Battery lock pin clockwise, and pull the Battery lever down. Confirm that a gap opens between the battery and the battery connector as the handle is pulled down. Turn the Battery lock pin counterclockwise to lock the Battery lever.

<image: "unlock\_battery\_connected.png"> <image: "disconnect\_battery.png">

     * Hardware 2.0 and higher only

Pull the Battery lock pin out while pulling the Battery lever down. Confirm that a gap opens between the battery and the battery connector as the handle is pulled down.

<image: "250\_mkII\_disconnect\_battery.png">

## Replacing the front and rear status light LED bands

  1. Unplug the status light connector.

<image: "250\_unplug\_front\_status\_light\_connector.png">

  2. Unscrew the two M3 nuts holding the status light LED band in place, and remove it from the robot..

<image: "250\_remove\_LED\_band\_screws\_front.png">

<image: "250\_remove\_LED\_band\_front.png">

  3. Mount the new status light LED band. Tighten the M3 nuts to 0.3 Nm.

  4. Reconnect the status light connector.

## Replacing the side status light LED bands

  1. Turn the two screws counterclockwise on the side cover, and remove the cover from the robot. Use a T30 bit.

<image: "side\_unscrew.png">

  2. Unplug the status light connector.

<image: "250\_unplug\_side\_status\_light\_connector.png">

  3. Unscrew the two M3 nuts holding the status light LED band in place, and remove it from the robot.

<image: "250\_remove\_LED\_band\_screws\_side.png">

<image: "250\_remove\_LED\_band\_side.png">

  4. Mount the new status light LED band. Tighten the M3 nuts to 0.3 Nm.

  5. Reconnect the status light connector.

## Testing

  1. Turn on the robot and verify that all four status light LED bands light up evenly.

  2. Closely inspect each LED band to verify that all LEDs light up.

  3. Test various statuses and verify that the correct colors are shown. See the manual for your application for more information on status light colors.
