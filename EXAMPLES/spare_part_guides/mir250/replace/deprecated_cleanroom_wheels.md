Failure to use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) can result in serious injury.

Determine relevant and necessary PPE based on the task, environment, and experience of personnel. The following is a list of common PPE you must consider:

  * Safety shoes to protect from impact or puncture hazards.

  * Safety glasses and face shield to protect from flying debris or chemical agents.

  * Hard hat to protect from falling or flying objects.

  * Gloves to protect against sharp objects, electrical hazards, chemical agents, and extreme temperatures.

  * Ear plugs or earmuffs to protect from high noise hazards.

  * Dust mask or respirator to protect from inhaling chemical agents or other harmful airborne hazards.

Electrical components can be damaged by electrostatic discharge. Consider moving the robot to an ESD protected area and using ESD protected clothing and tools.

This guide describes how to replace the deprecated cleanroom wheels on MiR250 (order number 130039) with the new cleanroom wheels (order number 130251).

If you have a MiR250 without cleanroom wheels and want to install cleanroom wheels, see the guide How to install cleanroom wheels on MiR250. You can find this document on [MiR Support Portal](https://supportportal.mobile-industrial-robots.com).

If you already have a MiR250 with the new cleanroom wheels installed and want to replace them, see the guide How to replace the cleanroom wheels on MiR250. You can find this document on [MiR Support Portal](https://supportportal.mobile-industrial-robots.com).

The clean room wheels are in compliance with ISO class 4 (from ISO 14644-1:2015).

## Things you will need

  * The cleanroom wheel kit from MiR for MiR250

  * A screwdriver with the following exchangeable bits:

    * T10

    * T30

    * T45

  * A 10 mm wrench

  * 2 × ½” socket wrench with 19 mm socket

  * A ½" torque wrench ranging from 25 Nm to 60 Nm

  * The ¼ inch bit adapter and T8 bit that was supplied with the deprecated cleanroom wheels

  * Support blocks to keep the robot lifted

## Kit contents

  * 4 × cleanroom wheels with footguards

  * 1 × 8 mm extra short arm Hex key

  * 1 × crowfoot adapter

  * An 8 mm extra short arm hex key or shortened to 20–25 mm

  * 2 × M12×30 hex-head bolts for the rear

  * 2 × M12×30 round head Allen key bolt for the front

## Before installing the cleanroom wheels

  1. Remove the rear cover by pressing the two white buttons and pulling off the cover. When removing the cover, un-click the two bottom corners first and then the two top corners.

<image: "rear\_cover\_buttons\_2.png"> <image: "rear\_cover\_remove\_2.png">

  2. Turn the Battery lever lock clockwise to unlock the battery connector.

<image: "unlock\_battery\_connected.png">

  3. Pull the Battery lever down to disconnect the battery.

<image: "disconnect\_battery.png">

  4. Turn the Battery lock switch counterclockwise to lock the battery connector again.

<image: "lock\_battery\_disconnected.png">

  5. Disconnect the antennas. Unscrew the antennas from the top compartment covers and remove them.

<image: "antenna\_unscrew.png">

  6. Disconnect the antenna cables from the top cover. Unscrew the two nuts from the antenna connectors. Use a 10 mm wrench. Then, push the antenna connectors through the covers.

<image: "antenna\_bolt\_unscrew.png">

  7. Remove the top compartment covers from the top plate by unscrewing the eight screws. Use a T10 screwdriver. Make sure the antenna cables are not connected to the compartment covers.

<image: "top\_unscrew.png"> <image: "top\_remove.png">

  8. Remove the top cover from the robot by unscrewing the four screws. Use a T45 screwdriver. Make sure the antenna cables are not connected to the top cover.

<image: "remove\_top\_cover\_screws.png"> <image: "remove\_top\_plate.png">

  9. Unscrew the two screws holding the front cover. Use a T30 screwdriver.

<image: "front\_unscrew.png">

  10. Loosen the bottom corners one at the time by pulling out each corner.

<image: "front\_bottom\_corners.png">

  11. Loosen the top corners one at the time by pulling each corner down, and then out.

<image: "front\_top\_corners.png">

  12. Pull off the cover.

<image: "front\_remove.png">

Remove the two side covers. To do that, follow these steps:

  13. Turn the two screws on the side cover counterclockwise. Use a T30 screwdriver.

<image: "side\_unscrew.png">

  14. Loosen the bottom corners one at the time by pulling out each corner.

<image: "side\_bottom\_corners.png">

  15. Pull off the cover.

<image: "side\_remove.png">

## Installing the rear cleanroom wheels

  1. Raise the robot from the ground using a lifting device so either or both of the bogies are raised from the ground and are not supporting the robot.

Placing the robot so it is resting on its charging plates can damage the plates.

     * Make sure the charging plates beneath the robot are not supporting the robot's weight.

<image: "on\_block\_cleanroom.png">

  2. Unscrew the two screws in the foot guard. Use a T8 bit and the ¼ inch bit adapter that was supplied with the deprecated cleanroom wheels. Remove the foot guard.

<image: "foot\_guard.png">

  3. Unscrew the deprecated cleanroom wheels, and remove them from the robot. Use a socket wrench with a 19 mm socket to unscrew the bolt at the bottom and another socket wrench to counter hold on top of the bolt if needed.

<image: "back\_wrench.png">

  4. Unscrew the three bolts holding the rear wheel mount brace. Use a T30 screwdriver. Remove the brace from the robot.

<image: "brace.png">

  5. Unscrew the three screws holding the rear wheel mount brace. Use a T45 screwdriver. Remove the mount from the robot.

<image: "remove\_mount1.png"> <image: "remove\_mount2.png">

  6. Replace the long bolt that was holding the caster wheel in place with a M12×30 hex-head bolt.

<image: "screw\_back 1.png">

  7. Remount the rear wheel mount brace to the robot. Use the three T45 bolts. Tighten the bolts to 25 Nm.

<image: "remove\_mount2.png">

  8. Fasten the new cleanroom wheel. Use the supplied crowfoot adapter and a torque wrench. Tighten the nut to 60 Nm.

<image: "mount cleanroom\_rear.png">

  9. Repeat from step 2 with the other rear cleanroom wheel.

## Replacing the front cleanroom wheels

  1. Unscrew the two screws in the foot guard. Use a T8 bit and the ¼ inch bit adapter that was supplied with the deprecated cleanroom wheels. Remove the foot guard.

<image: "foot\_guard.png">

  2. Unscrew the deprecated cleanroom wheels, and remove them from the robot. Use a socket wrench with a 19 mm socket to remove the nut and, if needed, an 8 mm extra short arm Hex key to counter hold the bolt.

<image: "front\_wrench.png">

To be able to exchange the wheel bolt, you need to take off the bogie and rest it next to the robot. To do this, follow these steps:

  3. Apply a lever or lifting device on the rear edge of the bogie to raise the bogie.

<image: "boogie\_raise.png">

  4. Remove the circlip on the bogie axle using the circlip pliers. The circlip keeps the bogie mounted to the axle. To ease the removal, it can be beneficial to hit the shaft on the opposite side of the robot with a rubber mallet.

     * Make sure the bogie arm is supported so it does not slide or tip over and causes damage to the cables or robot components.

     * Avoid overstretching the circlip so you can reuse it when mounting the bogie arm back on the robot.

<image: "circlip.png">

  5. Remove the washer behind the circlip.

<image: "washer.png">

  6. Gently pull the bogie arm off the axle, and rest it next to the robot. You should now have access to the top of the front wheel mount.

<image: "cleanroom\_bogie\_off.png">

  7. Replace the long bolt with a M12x30 mm round head Allen key bolt in the front wheel mount.

<image: "long screw.png">

  8. Fasten the wheel. Use the supplied crowfoot adapter and a torque wrench. Tighten to 60 Nm.

<image: "mount cleanroom\_front.png">

  9. Remount the bogie on the axle by following step 5 to step 7 in the reverse order.

  10. Repeat from step 2 with the other front cleanroom wheel.
