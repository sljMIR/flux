Failure to use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) can result in serious injury.

Determine relevant and necessary PPE based on the task, environment, and experience of personnel. The following is a list of common PPE you must consider:

*   Safety shoes to protect from impact or puncture hazards.
    
*   Safety glasses and face shield to protect from flying debris or chemical agents.
    
*   Hard hat to protect from falling or flying objects.
    
*   Gloves to protect against sharp objects, electrical hazards, chemical agents, and extreme temperatures.
    
*   Ear plugs or earmuffs to protect from high noise hazards.
    
*   Dust mask or respirator to protect from inhaling chemical agents or other harmful airborne hazards.

Electrical components can be damaged by electrostatic discharge. Consider moving the robot to an ESD protected area and using ESD protected clothing and tools.

To replace the robot computer on MiR600 and MiR1350, you need to temporarily install drawer extensions on the robot to be able to pull the front compartment drawer out far enough to access the robot computer. The extensions are included in the kit.

## Things you will need

*   A new robot computer kit for MiR600 and MiR1350 from MiR

*   A T10 and T30 screwdriver
    
*   A flat-head screwdriver

## Kit contents

*   1 × DFI robot computer
    
*   4 × self-adhesive M5 plastic washers
    
*   2 × drawer extensions

## Before mounting the extensions

**Pull open the right side hatch by pressing the two buttons on the hatch.**

<image: "press_hatch_buttons.png"> <image: "open_hatch.png">

**Disconnect the status light cable.**

<image: "disconnect_light.png">

**Turn the two hinges in the bottom corners to release the hatch, and remove the hatch from the robot.**

<image: "turn_hatch_hinges.png"> <image: "remove_side_hatch.png">

Turn the two screws on the front compartment hatch 90˚, and pull out the electronics drawer.

<image: "access_front_compartment_1.png"> <image: "access_front_compartment_2.png">

**Press the Emergency stop button on the front-right corner cover**. After reassembling, remember to release the button again by turning it counterclockwise.

<image: "Press_button.png">

**Remove the four screws from the front-right corner cover using a T30 screwdriver.** When reattaching the corner covers, tighten the screws to 11.6 Nm.

<image: "unscrew_right_screws 2.png"> <image: "unscrew_right_screws_1.png">

**Disconnect any last cables connected to the corner cover.**

**Remove the corner cover.** It may require some force to disconnect the shield from the corner bumper. Pull it downward firmly to remove it from the bumper.

<image: "corner_remove 1.png">

## Mounting the extensions

Make sure the extension arms are not folded out yet.

**Mount the right extension on the right side using the mounting holes and screws for the corner cover.**

<image: "mount_right_extension.png">

**Unscrew the two front screws for the front-left corner cover.**

<image: "unscrews_left screws.png">

**Mount the left extension on the left side using the mounting holes and screws for the corner cover.**

<image: "mount_left_extension.png">

## Using the extensions

**Pull out the black splints on the drawer rails on both sides.**

<image: "Pull_splint.png">

**Carefully, pull out the front compartment even further while folding out the two extension arms.**

<image: "pull_out_further 1.png">

## Replacing the robot computer

**Unplug all of the cables from the robot computer.**

**Loosen the four screws mounting the computer to the bracket using a T10 screwdriver.**

<image: "0026.png">

**Remove the computer from the bracket.**

<image: "PC0059.png">

Mount the four self-adhesive plastic washers around the four mounting holes on the back of the new robot computer.

<image: "washers.png">

**Move the four sets of screws and spacers from the old computer to the new computer.** Insert them loosely into the new computer.

<image: "0028.png">

Place the new computer in the bracket where the old computer was.

<image: "PC0061.png">

**Tighten the screws to 0.4 Nm.**

<image: "PC0060.png">

**Reconnect all of the cables.** The cables are labeled according to which socket each one must be connected to.

<image: "cable_overview.png">

**Push the front compartment drawer back onto the rails and remove the extensions by following the steps in** [Mounting the extensions](#Mounting) **in reverse.**

**USB restore your robot by following the guide _How to USB restore MiR600 and MiR1350_.**
