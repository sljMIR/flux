Failure to use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) can result in serious injury.

Determine relevant and necessary PPE based on the task, environment, and experience of personnel. The following is a list of common PPE you must consider:

*   Safety shoes to protect from impact or puncture hazards.
    
*   Safety glasses and face shield to protect from flying debris or chemical agents.
    
*   Hard hat to protect from falling or flying objects.
    
*   Gloves to protect against sharp objects, electrical hazards, chemical agents, and extreme temperatures.
    
*   Ear plugs or earmuffs to protect from high noise hazards.
    
*   Dust mask or respirator to protect from inhaling chemical agents or other harmful airborne hazards.

Electrical components can be damaged by electrostatic discharge. Consider moving the robot to an ESD protected area and using ESD protected clothing and tools.

## Things you will need

*   A torque wrench ranging from 1.2 to 90 Nm with the following exchangeable bits:
    
*   PH2
        
*   4 mm hex
        
*   22 mm hex socket
        
*   T25
        
*   T30
        
*   T45
        
*   T50
        
*   Support blocks

## Removing the covers and disconnecting the battery

1.  Press the two buttons on the left side hatch, and pull open the hatch.
    
    <image: "press_hatch_buttons.png"> <image: "open_hatch.png">
    
2.  Disconnect the status light cable.
    
    <image: "disconnect_light.png">
    
3.  Turn the two hinges in the bottom corners to release the hatch, and remove the hatch from the robot.
    
    <image: "turn_hatch_hinges.png">
    
4.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position.
    
    <image: "Disconnect_v2.png">
    
5.  Press the two buttons on the rear hatch, and pull open the hatch.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_1.png"> <image: "access_rear_compartment_2.png">
    
6.  Disconnect the status light cable.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_3.png">
    
7.  Turn the two hinges in the bottom corners to release the hatch, and remove the hatch from the robot.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_4.png">
    
8.  Remove the seven screws on the rear cover using a T25 screwdriver. When reassembling, tighten these screws to 5 Nm.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_6.png">
    
9.  Loosen the top three screws while holding the bottom of the cover. When reassembling, tighten these screws to 5 Nm.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_7.png">
    
10.  Remove the cover by sliding it downwards and out to access the rear maintenance compartment.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_8.png">

## Removing the top plate and corner cover

1.  Unscrew the six M8 screws and four M10 screws in the top plate. Use a T45 bit for the M8 and a T50 bit for the M10 screws. Tighten all 10 screws to 11.6 Nm when reassembling the robot.
    
    <image: "top_cover_all_screws.png"> <image: "top_cover_remove_screws_no_covers.png">
    
2.  Remove the top plate from the robot. Use a crane or be at least two people to lift it.
    
    <image: "top_cover_remove.png"> <image: "top_cover_remove_cover_no_covers.png">
    
3.  Remove the rear-left corner cover:
    
1.  Press the Emergency stop button on the rear-left corner. After reassembling, remember to release the button again.
        
2.  Remove the four screws from the corner cover. Use a T30 bit. When reattaching the corner covers, tighten the screws to 11.6 Nm.
        
        <image: "corn_rear_left_rem_screws_no_top.png">
        
3.  Disconnect the main proximity board cable harness connector within the side compartment end that is closest to the corner cover you are removing.
        
        <image: "disconnect_main_prox_harn_conn_no_top.png">
        
4.  Remove the corner cover.
        
        <image: "corner_shield_remove_no_top.png">

You may also have to repeat this process with the right-rear corner cover, if you are not able to reach the motor cables on the right-side bogie in [Unplugging cables](#Unpluggi).

## Pulling out the bogie

1.  Raise the robot above ground, and use a support block under the chassis and drive wheels to keep it lifted—see the section _Transportation_ in the manual for instructions on how to lift the robot safely.
    
    <image: "lift_robot_w_out_top.png">
    
2.  Remove the bogie screw. Use a 22 mm hex socket. When fastening the new bogie, tighten the screw to 90 Nm.
    
    <image: "bogie_unscrew_bogie_bolt_no_top.png">
    
3.  Pull out the wheel and bogie arm slightly. Pull out the bogie arm enough to make it possible to gain access behind the wheel gearing. Keep the drive wheel supported.  
    
    <image: "bogie_pull_out_bogie_no_top.png">

## Unplugging cables

1.  For each bogie, unplug the two cables connected directly to the motor. Turn the cable lock counter-clockwise to unlock the connector. The angled connector is sensitive. Be careful while handling it.
    
    If you cannot reach the connectors for the right-side bogie, repeat [Pulling out the bogie](#Pulling) with the other bogie to gain access.
    
    <image: "disconnect_motor_cables.png">
    
2.  Pull the cable out of the cable clips behind the gearbox.
    
    <image: "cable_clips.png">
    
3.  In the rear compartment, unplug the wires connected to terminals 2, 4, and 6 in the K4 and K5 contactors. Use a PH2 bit to loosen the screws to release the wires. When reconnecting the wires, tighten the screws to 1.2 Nm.
    
    <image: "disconnect_k4_k5_cables.png">
    
4.  Unplug the cables in ports J11, J12 , J14, and J15 in the motor controller carrier board.
    
    <image: "disconnect_motor_controller_cables.png">

## Removing the harness

1.  Remove the top of the cable entry frame by unscrewing the top two screws. Use a 4 mm hex bit.
    
    <image: "remove_top_from_rear_cable_holder.png">
    
2.  Pull out the two cable grommets. If you need more clearance for the cable in the rear compartment, loose the four screws in the frame. Use a T25 bit. Tighten the screws to 2.6 Nm when reassembling.
    
    <image: "remove_rear_cable_holders.png">
    
3.  On the other side of the compartment, remove the tops of the two cable grommets to the motor compartment. Slide the tops away from compartment wall to remove them.
    
    <image: "remove_center_comp_cable_lids.png">
    
4.  Gently pull the cables out of the grommets and through the hole above. Remove the complete cable harness from the robot.
    
    <image: "remove_cable.png">

## Replacing the cable harness

1.  Remove the small single grommets and the rear compartment entry frame and gaskets from the new cable harness.
    
*   If the grommets or gaskets currently in the robot is damaged, remove the damaged part from the robot and use the new grommet or gasket and entry frame included with the new harness. You do not have to remove the new part from the harness when reinstalling them.
        
*   If you are removing entry frame and gaskets from the robot, be aware that this part is glued to the robot and requires some force to remove. There is a risk of damaging the part while removing it.

    <image: "remove_cable_parts.png">
    
2.  Feed the cable harness through the compartment grommets and fix the rubber grommets into the entry frame by following the steps in [Removing the harness](#Removing) in reverse.
    
    The protective tube is tightly fitted in the motor compartment grommets. Some force is required when inserting the cable tube. Do not use sharp or thin tools, like a flat head screwdriver, to help push the tube in place. This may break the protective tube.
    
    Make sure to fit the grommet around the same part of the protective tube that the original grommet was removed from.
    
3.  Plug the cables in to their sockets by following the steps in [Unplugging cables](#Unpluggi) in reverse.
    
4.  Reassemble the robot by following the steps in [Pulling out the bogie](#Pulling), [Removing the top plate and corner cover](#Removing2), and [Removing the covers and disconnecting the battery](#Removing3) in reverse.
