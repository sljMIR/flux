Failure to use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) can result in serious injury.

Determine relevant and necessary PPE based on the task, environment, and experience of personnel. The following is a list of common PPE you must consider:

*   Safety shoes to protect from impact or puncture hazards.
    
*   Safety glasses and face shield to protect from flying debris or chemical agents.
    
*   Hard hat to protect from falling or flying objects.
    
*   Gloves to protect against sharp objects, electrical hazards, chemical agents, and extreme temperatures.
    
*   Ear plugs or earmuffs to protect from high noise hazards.
    
*   Dust mask or respirator to protect from inhaling chemical agents or other harmful airborne hazards.

Electrical components can be damaged by electrostatic discharge. Consider moving the robot to an ESD protected area and using ESD protected clothing and tools.

This guide explains how to replace the top plate on MiR600 and MiR1350. The top plate spare part kit includes new bumpers that are preinstalled.

## Things you will need

*   A T20 screwdriver
    
*   An 11.6 Nm torque wrench for the following:
    
*   A T45 head
        
*   A 6 mm hex socket

## Before replacing the top plate

1.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position.

<image: "Disconnect_v2.png">

1.  Remove the two top compartment covers from the old top plate using a T20 screwdriver. The top compartment covers will be reused on the new top plate. Save the eight M4 screws for reassembly.  

<image: "top_cover_all_screws.png">

<image: "top_cover_remove.png">

## Replacing the top plate

1.  Remove the old top plate.
    
1.  Unscrew the six M8 screws using a wrench with a T45 head.
        
2.  Unscrew the four M10 screws using a wrench with a 6 mm hex socket.  

    <image: "remove_top_plate_no_top_hatches.png">
    
2.  Lift the top plate off of the robot. It is recommended to have two persons lift the top plate.  
    
    <image: "lifting_the_top_plate_no_top_hatches.png">
    
3.  Carefully place the new top plate on the robot, and align it with the mounting holes. It is recommended to have two persons lift the top plate.
    
    There are no front and back on the top plate. It is symmetrical in design, which means it will fit correctly in either orientation.
    
4.  Reinsert the six M8 screws in the top plate. Tighten them to 11.6 Nm using a torque wrench with a T45 head.
    
5.  Reinsert the four M10 screws in the top plate. Tighten them to 11.6 Nm using a torque wrench with a 6 mm hex socket.
    
6.  Mount the two top compartment covers on the new top plate using the eight M4 screws. Tighten them to 2.5 Nm using a T20 screwdriver.
    
7.  Reconnect the battery, and power up the robot.
