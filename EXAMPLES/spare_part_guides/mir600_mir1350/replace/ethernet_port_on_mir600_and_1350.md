Failure to use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) can result in serious injury.

Determine relevant and necessary PPE based on the task, environment, and experience of personnel. The following is a list of common PPE you must consider:

*   Safety shoes to protect from impact or puncture hazards.
    
*   Safety glasses and face shield to protect from flying debris or chemical agents.
    
*   Hard hat to protect from falling or flying objects.
    
*   Gloves to protect against sharp objects, electrical hazards, chemical agents, and extreme temperatures.
    
*   Ear plugs or earmuffs to protect from high noise hazards.
    
*   Dust mask or respirator to protect from inhaling chemical agents or other harmful airborne hazards.

Electrical components can be damaged by electrostatic discharge. Consider moving the robot to an ESD protected area and using ESD protected clothing and tools.

## Things you will need

*   Flat-head screwdriver
    
*   T30 bit
    
*   T10 bit
    
*   A torque wrench with exchangeable bits that ranges from 0.3–11.6 Nm

## Before replacing the Ethernet port

1.  Turn the two screws on the front compartment hatch 90˚. Use a flat-head screwdriver.
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_1.png">
    
2.  Pull out the electronics drawer.
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_2.png">
    
3.  Press the two buttons on the left side hatch.
    
    <image: "press_hatch_buttons.png">
    
4.  Pull open the hatch.
    
    <image: "open_hatch.png">
    
5.  Disconnect the status light cable.
    
    <image: "disconnect_light.png">
    
6.  Turn the two hinges in the bottom corners to release the hatch.
    
    <image: "turn_hatch_hinges.png">
    
7.  Remove the hatch from the robot.
    
    <image: "remove_side_hatch.png">
    
8.  Press the Emergency stop button on the front right corner cover. After reassembling, remember to release the button again.
    
9.  Remove the four screws from the corner cover using a T30 screwdriver. When reattaching the corner covers, tighten the screws to 11.6 Nm.
    
    <image: "access_corner_shield_1.png">
    
10.  Remove the corner cover. It may require some force to disconnect the shield from the corner bumper. Pull it downward firmly to remove it from the bumper.
    
    <image: "access_corner_shield_3.png">

## Replacing the Ethernet port

1.  Disconnect the Ethernet cable from the port.
    
    <image: "disconnect_ethernet_cable.png">
    
2.  Loosen the screws securing the port to the bracket using a T10 screwdriver, and discard the port. Keep the screws.
    
    <image: "loosen_screws.png">
    
3.  Use the two T10 screws to mount the new connector. Make sure that the RJ45 clip cutout is oriented downwards. Tighten the screws to 0.3 Nm.
    
    <image: "tighten_screws.png">
    
4.  Reconnect the Ethernet cable to the port, and reassemble the robot.

_Product Service Note - New and more stable Ethernet port for MiR600 and MiR1350 robots_
