## Things you will need

*   A 10 mm wrench
    
*   A 27 mm wrench
    
*   A screwdriver or wrench with the following exchangeable bits:
    
*   T30
        
*   4 mm hex
        
*   Flat-head

## Kit contents

2 × Intel D435 3D camera

## Before replacing the 3D camera

1.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position.
    
    <image: "Disconnect_v2.png">
    
2.  Turn the two screws on the front compartment hatch 90˚. Use a flat-head bit.
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_1.png">
    
3.  Pull out the electronics drawer.
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_2.png">
    
4.  Remove the three screws holding the front of the drawer. Use a 10 mm wrench.
    
    <image: "1_remove_screws_front_plate_MiR600_MiR1350.png">
    
5.  Unplug the front status light connector.
    
    <image: "2_remove_front_LED_cable_MiR600_MiR1350.png">
    
6.  Unplug the two USB cables from the 3D cameras.
    
    <image: "3_remove_the_USB-cable_for_cameras.png">
    
7.  Remove the electronics drawer front.
    
    <image: "4_remove_electronics_drawer_front.png">

## Replacing the 3D camera

1.  Unscrew the two screws from the electronics drawer front. Use a T30 bit.
    
    <image: "e-drawer_backplate_unscrew.png">
    
2.  Unscrew the locks of the electronics drawer. When reassembling make sure to face the locks as they were before. Use a 10 mm wrench.
    
    <image: "600_1350_e-drawer_backplate_unscrew.png">
    
3.  Remove the housing of the electronics drawer locks from the backplate. Use a 27 mm wrench.
    
    <image: "600_1350_e-drawer_backplate_remove_nuts.png">
    
4.  Separate the backplate from the front of the electronics drawer.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_e-drawer_backplate.png">
    
5.  Remove the four nuts holding the 3D camera housing in place. Use a 10 mm wrench.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_3D_camera_housing.png">
    
6.  Unscrew the screw holding the camera that you need to replace. Use a 4 mm hex key.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_left_3D_camera.png">
    
7.  Remove the 3D camera that you are replacing from the housing.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_both_cameras.png">
    
8.  Reassemble the robot by following this guide in reverse.

## Testing

Test the new 3D camera as described in How to test if the 3D cameras are working on MiR robots. You can find this guide on [MiR Support Portal.](https://supportportal.mobile-industrial-robots.com)

## Troubleshooting

If the new 3D camera is not working as intended, it may be necessary to either calibrate the camera and update its firmware. See How to calibrate a D435 3D camera. You can find this guide on [MiR Support Portal.](https://supportportal.mobile-industrial-robots.com)
