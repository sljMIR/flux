Determine relevant and necessary PPE based on the task, environment, and experience of personnel. The following is a list of common PPE you must consider:

*   Safety shoes to protect from impact or puncture hazards.
    
*   Safety glasses and face shield to protect from flying debris or chemical agents.

## Things you will need

*   The rear bogie kit from MiR
    
*   A torque wrench ranging from 11.6 to 90 Nm with the following exchangeable bits
    
*   T20
        
*   13 mm hex socket
        
*   22 mm hex socket
        
*   A lifting device to raise the robot
    
*   Support blocks to place the robot on

## Kit contents

*   1 × rear bogie
    
*   1 × washer
    
*   1 × M14 locknut
    
*   8 × M8 locknuts

## Before replacing the rear bogie

1.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position.
    
    <image: "Disconnect_v2.png">
    
2.  Lift the robot onto a secure structure where you can safely access the area under the front compartment. To ensure the robot is not damaged when lifting it, see the manual for your robot application for lifting specifications.

    If you are under the robot while it is lifted, you risk serious injury if the robot falls or slips.
    
*   Stand clear of the robot while it is raised in the air.
        
*   Only approach the robot when it is stationed on a secure structure.
        
*   See the section about lifting the robot in the manual for your robot application.

    <image: "mir600_cooling_element0004.png">
    
3.  Press the two buttons on the rear hatch, and pull open the hatch.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_1.png"> <image: "access_rear_compartment_2.png">
    
4.  Disconnect the status light cable.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_3.png">
    
5.  Turn the two hinges in the bottom corners to release the hatch, and remove the hatch from the robot.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_4.png">
    
6.  Press the two buttons on the side hatch, and pull open the hatch.
    
    <image: "press_hatch_buttons.png"> <image: "open_hatch.png">
    
7.  Disconnect the status light cable.
    
    <image: "disconnect_light.png">
    
8.  Turn the two hinges in the bottom corners to release the hatch, and remove the hatch from the robot.
    
    <image: "turn_hatch_hinges.png">

## Remove the corner covers

For each of two rear corner covers, follow these steps: 

1.  Press the Emergency stop buttons. After reassembling, remember to release the button again.
    
2.  Remove the four screws from the corner cover. Use a T30 bit. When reattaching the corner cover, tighten the screws to 11.6 Nm.
    
    <image: "access_corner_shield_1.png">
    
3.  Disconnect the main proximity board cable harness connector within the side compartment end that is closest to the corner cover you are removing.
    
    <image: "disconnect_main_proxy_harness_connector.png">
    
4.  Remove the corner cover. It may require some force to disconnect the cover from the corner bumper. Pull it downward firmly to remove it from the bumper.
    
    <image: "corner_remove 1.png">

## Replacing the rear bogie

1.  Remove the four screws from the two side panels and remove the panels. Use a T30 bit.
    
    <image: "remove_side_panels.png">
    
2.  Remove the locknut from the rear bogie axle. Use a 22 mm hex socket or wrench. Discard the nut.
    
    <image: "remove_lock_nut.png">
    
3.  Remove the rear bogie, spacer, and washer. Discard the washer. Keep the spacer.
    
    <image: "remove_bogie_washer_spacer.png">
    
4.  Move the two caster wheels from the old bogie to the new bogie. Discard the old M8 locknuts and use the locknuts from the kit when mounting the wheels to the new bogie. Tighten the nuts to 15 Nm. Use a 13 mm hex socket or wrench.
    
    <image: "mount_wheels.png">
    
    <image: "mount_wheel_nuts.png">
    
5.  Slide the new washer, followed by the old spacer, and new bogie onto the axle. Face the bogie so the thicker end is toward the robot.
    
    <image: "mount_bogie_washer_spacer.png">
    
6.  Mount the new M14 locknut after the bogie. Tighten the nut to 90 Nm. Use a 22 mm hex socket or wrench.
    
    <image: "mount_lock_nut.png">
    
7.  Mount the two side panels by reusing the four M6 screws. Use a T30 bit. Tighten the screws to 11.6 Nm.
    
    <image: "mount_side_panels.png">

## Reassembly

Reassemble the robot by following the steps in [Remove the corner covers](#Remove) and [Before replacing the rear bogie](#Before) in reverse.
