# How to replace the antenna cable between the top interface and the robot computer on MiR600 and MiR1350

## Things you will need

*   A 10 mm wrench
*   An 8 mm wrench
*   A torque wrench ranging from 5 to 11.6 Nm with the following exchangeable bits:
*   T30
*   T45
*   T50
*   Flat-head screwdriver
*   A pair of cutting pliers

## Kit contents

1 × antenna cable—RP-SMA straight female connector to RP-SMA angled male connector

## Before replacing the antenna cable between the top interface and the robot computer

1.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position.
    
    <image: "Disconnect_v2.png">
    
2.  Unscrew the six M8 screws and four M10 screws in the top plate. Use a T45 bit for the M8 and a T50 bit for the M10 screws. Tighten all 10 screws to 11.6 Nm when reassembling the robot.
    
    <image: "top_cover_all_screws.png">
    
3.  Remove the top plate from the robot. We recommend having two persons lifting the top plate.
    
    <image: "top_cover_remove.png">
    
4.  Remove the front compartment cover by unscrewing the six M6×10 screws. Use a T30 bit. When reassembling, tighten these screws to 5 Nm.
    
    <image: "front_compartment_top_screws.png"> <image: "front_copmartment_top_remove.png">
    
5.  Turn the two screws on the front compartment hatch 90˚, and pull out the electronics drawer.
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_1.png"> <image: "access_front_compartment_2.png">

## Replacing the antenna cable between the top compartment and the robot computer

1.  Unscrew the two M6 nuts holding the top interface bracket. Use a 10 mm wrench.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_top_interface_bracket.png">
    
2.  Push the top interface bracket backwards to remove it from the frame.
    
    <image: "600_1350_push_top_interface_bracket.png">
    
3.  Unscrew the nut holding the RP-SMA female antenna connector, and remove the connector from the top interface bracket. Use an 8 mm wrench.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_antenna_top_interface.png">
    
4.  Insert the new cable's straight RP-SMA antenna connector into the top interface bracket and tighten it in place. Use an 8 mm wrench.
    
5.  Disconnect the old antenna cable from the robot computer. Use an 8 mm wrench. You will have to remove some cable ties in order to disconnect the cable. Use a pair of cutting pliers or similar.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_antenna_cable_robot_computer.png">
    
6.  Route the new antenna cable as illustrated, and connect it to the robot computer.
    
    <image: "600_1350_top_interface_antenna_cable_replace.png">
    
7.  Tie the new antenna cable onto a nearby cable harness on the suggested cable routing using cable ties. Include enough slack for the electronics drawer to fully open.
    
8.  Reassemble the robot by following [Replacing the antenna cable between the top compartment and the robot computer](#Replacin) from [step 2](#Push) in reverse, and then follow [Before replacing the antenna cable between the top interface and the robot computer](#Before) in reverse.

## Testing

Test the new antenna cable connecting to the robot wirelessly after the intended top application has been remounted and connected to the top interface. Verify that there are no connection issues.

## Troubleshooting

If the robot experiences communication errors after replacing the antenna cable, verify that:

The new antenna cable is correctly inserted into the robot computer and that all connections are tightened correctly.
