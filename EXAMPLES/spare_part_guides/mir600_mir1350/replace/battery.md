# Replacing the battery on MiR250

## Before replacing the battery

1.  Turn off the robot by pressing and holding the Power button for three seconds.

## Replacing the battery

1.  Push the two buttons on the rear cover, and remove the cover from the robot.
    
    <image: "rear_press_buttons.png"> <image: "rear_remove.png">
    
2.  Disconnect the battery.
    
*   Hardware 1.0 only
        
        Turn the Battery lock pin clockwise, and pull the Battery lever down. Confirm that a gap opens between the battery and the battery connector as the handle is pulled down. Turn the Battery lock pin counterclockwise to lock the Battery lever.
        
        <image: "unlock_battery_connected.png"> <image: "disconnect_battery.png">
        
*   Hardware 2.0 and higher only
        
        Pull the Battery lock pin out while pulling the Battery lever down. Confirm that a gap opens between the battery and the battery connector as the handle is pulled down.
        
        <image: "250_mkII_disconnect_battery.png">
        
3.  Enable fast swap if you have not previously done so. See the manual for your robot application for more information.
    
4.  Gently pull the battery out.  

    <image: "pull_out_battery.png">
    
5.  Insert the replacement battery.
    
6.  Pull the Battery lever up.
    
*   On hardware 1.0, turn the Battery lock pin counterclockwise to lock the battery in place.
        
*   On hardware 2.0, verify that the Battery lock pin has clicked into place, locking the battery.
        
7.  Reattach the rear cover.

## Testing

Turn on the robot by pressing and holding the Power button for three seconds.

# Replacing the battery on MiR600 and MiR1350

## Before replacing the battery

1.  Turn off the robot by pressing and holding the Power button for three seconds.

## Replacing the battery

1.  Open the side hatch by pressing the two buttons on the left side of the robot, and pull open the hatch.  

    <image: "side_hatch_right_buttons.png"><image: "side_hatch_right_open.png">
    
2.  Disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position. <image: "Disconnect_v2.png">  
    
3.  Gently pull the battery out.  

    <image: "remove_battery.png">  
    
4.  Insert the replacement battery.
    
5.  Pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever up to the center position to lock the battery in place, or to the top position to lock and connect the battery to the robot.
    
6.  Close the side hatch.

## Testing

Turn on the robot by pressing and holding the Power button for five seconds.
