Failure to use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) can result in serious injury.

Determine relevant and necessary PPE based on the task, environment, and experience of personnel. The following is a list of common PPE you must consider:

*   Safety shoes to protect from impact or puncture hazards.
    
*   Safety glasses and face shield to protect from flying debris or chemical agents.
    
*   Hard hat to protect from falling or flying objects.
    
*   Gloves to protect against sharp objects, electrical hazards, chemical agents, and extreme temperatures.
    
*   Ear plugs or earmuffs to protect from high noise hazards.
    
*   Dust mask or respirator to protect from inhaling chemical agents or other harmful airborne hazards.

Electrical components can be damaged by electrostatic discharge. Consider moving the robot to an ESD protected area and using ESD protected clothing and tools.

## Things you will need

*   A screwdriver or wrench with the following exchangeable bits:
    
*   Flat-head
        
*   T30
        
*   A hammer and punch
    
*   A permanent marker
    
*   A small flat-head screwdriver
    
*   A 30 mm wrench
    
*   Two blocks of wood or similar to support the robot and bogie
    
*   Torque wrenches capable of 9 to 90 Nm with the following exchangeable bits and sockets:
    
*   8 mm hex bit
        
*   22 mm socket
        
*   30 mm socket

## Kit contents

*   4 × disc springs
    
*   2 × M20X1 drive wheel shaft nuts (for the front side drive shaft assembly)
    
*   2 × M20X1 set screws (for the front side drive shaft assembly)
    
*   2 × M17X1 drive wheel shaft nuts (for the rear side drive shaft assembly)
    
*   2 × M17X1 set screws (for the rear side drive shaft assembly)

## Before replacing the drive wheel shaft nuts

1.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position.
    
    <image: "Disconnect_v2.png">
    
2.  On the side with the bogie that you want to service, press the two buttons on the hatch.  
    
    <image: "access_side_compartment_1.png">
    
3.  Pull open the hatch.
    
    <image: "access_side_compartment_2.png">
    
4.  Disconnect the status light cable.
    
    <image: "access_side_compartment_3.png">
    
5.  Turn the two hinges in the bottom corners to release the hatch, and remove the hatch from the robot.  
    
    <image: "access_side_compartment_4.png">

## Accessing the front compartment

1.  Turn the two screws on the front compartment hatch 90˚ using a flat-head screwdriver.  
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_1.png">
    
2.  Pull out the electronics drawer.
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_2.png">

## Removing the corner cover

1.  Press the Emergency stop button on the corner cover you are going to remove. After reassembling, remember to release the button again.
    
2.  Remove the four screws from the corner cover using a T30 screwdriver. When reattaching the corner covers, tighten the screws to 11.6 Nm.  
    
    <image: "access_corner_shield_1.png">
    
3.  Remove the corner cover. It may require some force to dislodge the shield from the corner bumper. Pull it downward firmly to remove it.
    
    <image: "access_corner_shield_3.png">

## Pulling out the bogie arm

1.  Raise the robot above ground, and use a piece of wood or other firm material to keep it lifted—see the section _Lifting MiR600/MiR1350_ in the manual for instructions on how to lift the robot safely. The drive wheel must not be in contact with the floor.
    
    <image: "bogie_lift_robot.png">
    
2.  Remove the bogie bolt using a 22 mm hex socket. When fastening the bogie, tighten the bolt to 90 Nm.
    
    <image: "bogie_unscrew_bogie_bolt.png">
    
3.  Pull out the bogie enough to make it possible to access the cables connected to the motor.  
    
    <image: "bogie_pull_out_bogie.png">
    
4.  Disconnect the motor power cable and the hall sensor cable directly from the motor. The bogie arm with the motor is now detached. When reassembling, remember to mount the motor power cable in the two cable clips on the back of the gearbox.
    
    <image: "bogie_cables3.png">
    
5.  Pull the bogie outwards from the robot, so there is space to work on the back of the gearbox. Place support blocks under the bogie and motor to make sure they are stable and supported while you work on them.
    
    <image: "add_wood_block.png">

## Replacing the drive wheel shaft nut

Replace the rear part of the drive wheel shaft first, then repeat the steps in the section to replace the front part.

1.  Use a hammer and punch or a small pair of pliers to loosen the old KM nut and locking ring. Remove both.
    
    <image: "remove_KM4_KM3_bolt 1.png">
    
2.  Clean the threads of the shaft thoroughly with a degreasing agent and wipe them off with a paper towel. Even though the threads look clean, they can still be covered in an invisible oil film. It is important that the threads are clean before replacing the nuts.
    
3.  Mount the cone-shaped spring and the new drive wheel shaft nut. The cone-shaped spring must face with the top of cone towards the gearbox.
    
    <image: "add_nut_and_spring.png">
    
4.  Turn the nut by hand while keeping the spring centered. Use a small flat-head screwdriver to keep the spring centered while turning the nut. It is very important that the spring is completely centered before tightening the nut. Hand-tighten the nut so it just touches the spring. The spring should be fixed, but not under load.
    
    <image: "cross_section_view.png">
    
5.  Make sure that the spring is not visible on any side of the nut.
    
    <image: "spring_misaligned.png"> <image: "spring_correctly_aligned.png">
    
6.  When replacing the rear part, pull the shaft all the way in by tightening the nut to 9 Nm and then loosening it. Use a 30 mm socket. Skip this step when replacing the front part.
    
    <image: "tighten_nut.png">
    
    Untighten the nut so it is hand-tight, keeping the spring in place.  
    <image: "untighten_nut.png">  
    
    Confirm that the spring is still centered.
    
7.  Mark the starting point on the backplate of the gearbox with a permanent marker relative to the starting point groove (**A**) in the nut.
    
    When replacing the front part, mark the starting point on the wheel hub.
    
    <image: "mark_start_pen.png">
    
8.  Tighten the nut approx. 310° until the ending point groove (**B**) lines up with the marked starting point drawn in the previous step.
    
    <image: "tighten_to_starting_point.png">
    
9.  Screw in the M17 set screw into the drive wheel shaft nut. Note that the groove in the screw must point towards the shaft as illustrated.
    
    <image: "add_set_screw.png">
    
10.  Tighten the set screw to 60 Nm. Use a torque wrench with a 8 mm hex bit. Use a wrench to hold the nut in place while tightening the set screw. The nut must not turn with the set screw.
    
    <image: "tighten_set_screw.png">
    
11.  Mark the position of the set screw relative to the nut.
    
    <image: "mark_set_screw.png">
    
12.  Complete steps 1–5 and then 7–12 for the front of the wheel as well. Repeat the entire procedure for the other bogie.

## Reassembling the robot

1.  Repeat the steps in [Pulling out the bogie arm](#Removing) in reverse.
    
2.  Repeat the steps in [Removing the corner cover](#Removing_the_corner_shield) in reverse.
    
3.  Repeat the steps in [Before replacing the drive wheel shaft nuts](#Before_replacing_the_drive_wheel_shaft_nuts) in reverse.
