*   Equipment to lift the robot (minimum 1.000 kg)
    
*   A 10 mm wrench

*   T50
        
*   A pair of cutting pliers

## Kit contents

1 × power cable (harness 02)

## Overview

The power cable is routed from the electronics drawer, to the left-side top compartment, over the middle compartment, and down through the rear compartment. It is connected to the STO contactors K1 and K2, the power board, the charging module, the battery connector, and the motor controller carrier board.

You may need to cut some cable ties while unplugging the various connections of the power cable. Use a pair of cutting pliers or similar.

## Before replacing the power cable on MiR600 and MiR1350

### Disconnect the battery

1.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position. <image: "Disconnect_v2.png">

### Remove the top plate

1.  Unscrew the six M8 screws and four M10 screws in the top plate. Use a T45 bit for the M8 and a T50 bit for the M10 screws. Tighten all 10 screws to 11.6 Nm when reassembling the robot.
    
    <image: "top_cover_all_screws.png"> <image: "top_cover_remove_screws_no_covers.png">
    
2.  Remove the top plate from the robot. Use a crane or be at least two people to lift it.
    
    <image: "top_cover_remove.png"> <image: "top_cover_remove_cover_no_covers.png">

### Lift the robot

1.  Lift the robot onto a secure structure where you can safely access the area under the front compartment. To ensure the robot is not damaged when lifting it, see the manual for your robot application for lifting specifications.

    If you are under the robot while it is lifted, you risk serious injury if the robot falls or slips.
    
*   Stand clear of the robot while it is raised in the air.
        
*   Only approach the robot when it is stationed on a secure structure.
        
*   See the section about lifting the robot in the manual for your robot application.

    <image: "mir600_cooling_element0004.png">

### Access the front compartment

Turn the two screws on the front compartment hatch 90˚, and pull out the electronics drawer.

<image: "access_front_compartment_1.png"> <image: "access_front_compartment_2.png">

### Access the rear compartment

1.  Press the two buttons on the rear hatch.
    
2.  Pull open the rear hatch.
    
3.  Disconnect the status light cable.
    
4.  Turn the two hinges in the bottom corners to release the hatch, and remove the hatch from the robot.

5.  Remove the seven screws on the rear cover. Use a T25 bit. When reassembling, tighten these screws to 5 Nm.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_6.png">
    
6.  Loosen the three topmost screws while holding the bottom of the cover. When reassembling, tighten these screws to 5 Nm.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_7.png">
    
7.  Remove the cover by sliding it downwards and out to access the rear compartment.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_8.png">

## Replacing the power cable

### The left-side top compartment and the charging module

1.  Remove the front compartment cover by unscrewing the six M6×10 screws. Use a T30 bit. When reassembling, tighten these screws to 5 Nm.
    
    <image: "front_compartment_top_screws.png"> <image: "front_copmartment_top_remove.png">
    
2.  Unscrew the two M6 nuts holding the top interface bracket in the left-side top compartment. Use a 10 mm wrench. When reassembling, tighten to 11.6 Nm.
    
    <image: "600_1350_unscrew_top_interface_bracket.png">
    
3.  Push the top interface bracket backwards to remove it from the frame.
    
    <image: "600_1350_push_top_interface_bracket.png">
    
4.  Unscrew the four M3 x 10 screws holding the power connector, and remove the connector from the top interface bracket. Use a T10 bit. The screws will be reused.
    
    <image: "600_1350_unscrew_Power_connector.png">
    
5.  Insert the new cable's power connector into the top interface bracket. Ensure that the notch on the connector is turned upwards. Reuse the four screws and tighten them to 1.2 Nm.
    
    <image: "600_1350_Power_connector_up.png">
    
6.  Route the first part of the new power cable from the top compartment bracket to the charging module. See [Overview](#Overview) for the routing.
    
7.  Remove the caps from the red cable labeled + and the black cable labeled - in the charging module. The caps will be reused.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_caps_from_connectors.png">
    
8.  Unplug the two charging cables by unscrewing the two screws. Use a T25 bit. The screws will be reused.
    
    <image: "600_1350_disconnect_cables_from_charging_module.png">
    
9.  Insert the new charging cables into the charging module and position them like the old ones were positioned. Tighten the screws to 2.6 Nm. Remount the old caps to the terminals.

### The STO contactors K1 and K2 and the power board

1.  Route the next part of the new power cable from the charging module to the STO contactors and the power board. See [Overview](#Overview) for the routing.
    
2.  Unplug the wires K1\_1, K1\_3, K2\_1, and K2\_3 from the STO contactors K1 and K2 illustrated below.
    
    <image: "cable_overview_top_power_cable_harness.png">
    
3.  Plug in the new K1\_1, K1\_3, K2\_1, and K2\_3 wires from the new power cable.
    
4.  Unplug the cables J1 and J2 from the power board.  
    
    <image: "powerboard_overview_J1_J2.png">
    
5.  Plug in the cables J1 and J2 from the new power cable into the power board.

### The motor controller carrier board and the battery connector

1.  Route the new power cable from the STO contactors and the power board to the motor controller carrier board. See [Overview](#Overview) for the routing.
    
2.  Slide the bracket with the manual brake release switch and MiR remote interface toward you and unhook it from the robot. This makes it easier to access the carrier board. Loosen the screws on both ends. Make sure to not pull on any of the attached cables or wires.
    
    <image: "remove_switches.png">
    
3.  Unplug the J5 connector of the old power cable from the motor controller carrier board.  
    
    <image: "connectors_J5.png">
    
4.  Unplug the Interconnect connector from the main harness.  
    
    <image: "600_1350_disconnect_interconnect_MC_board.png">
    
5.  Remove the battery connector from the chassis by unscrewing the two K35 x 14 screws. Use a T10 bit. When reassembling, tighten to 0.9 Nm.
    
    <image: "remove_battery_connector_hw_2.1.png">
    
6.  Remove the battery connector cover by opening the lock. Use a screwdriver or similar. Gently, pull out the cables from the battery connector cover.
    
    <image: "600_1350_battery_connector.png">
    
7.  Remove the six screws from the grommet holder that holds two of the harnesses on the back of the rear compartment. Use a T25 bit. Gently, take off the top of the grommet holder, and pull out the power cable that is located on the left side.  
    
    <image: "600_1350_grommet_holder.png">
    
8.  Route the new power cable and its three connectors through the grommet holder and back to the battery connector, the Interconnect connector and the motor controller carrier board.
    
9.  Plug in the J5 connector of the new power cable into the motor controller carrier board.
    
10.  Plug in the the Interconnect connector of the new power cable to the main harness.
    
11.  Mount the battery connector cover to the new cables and mount the new battery connector of the new power cable to the chassis.

## Reassemble the robot

1.  Ensure that all connectors and wires are secured to their respective places. Tie the new power cable onto the main harness on the suggested cable routing using cable ties. Include enough slack for the electronics drawer to fully open.
    
2.  Reassemble the robot by following Step 1-3 in [The left-side top compartment and the charging module](#The), and [Before replacing the power cable on MiR600 and MiR1350](#Before) in reverse.
    
3.  Reconnect the battery, and turn on the robot.

## Testing

To verify that the new cable is mounted correctly, connect a device to the power connector in the top interface and confirm that there are no connection issues.
