## Things you will need

*   A torque wrench ranging from 1–11.6 Nm with the following bits: 
    
*   T30
        
*   T45
        
*   T50
        
*   8 mm open jaw
        
*   An 11 mm open jaw wrench

## Kit contents

*   1 × Bracket with Ethernet connector

## Before replace the Ethernet bracket

1.  Unscrew the six M8 screws and four M10 screws in the top plate. Use a T45 bit for the M8 and a T50 bit for the M10 screws. Tighten all 10 screws to 11.6 Nm when reassembling the robot.
    
    <image: "top_cover_all_screws.png"> <image: "top_cover_remove_screws_no_covers.png">
    
2.  Remove the top plate from the robot. Use a crane or be at least two people to lift it.
    
    <image: "top_cover_remove.png"> <image: "top_cover_remove_cover_no_covers.png">

## Replaceing the Ethernet bracket

1.  Unscrew the antenna from the bracket.
    
    <image: "remove_antenna_hw2.2.png">
    
2.  Unscrew the outer nut on the antenna connector and remove the nut and washer. Use an 11 mm wrench to hold the connector, and a 8 mm wrench to unscrew the nut.
    
    When reassembling, tighten the nut to 1 Nm.
    
    <image: "remove_antenna_nut_hw2.2.png">
    
3.  Pull out the antenna cable from the bracket, and unplug the Ethernet cable.
    
    <image: "disconnect_bracket_cables_hw2.2.png">
    
4.  Remove the two mounting screws. Use a T30 bit.
    
    <image: "remove_bracket_screws_hw2.2.png">
    
5.  Remove the bracket from the robot.
    
    <image: "remove_bracket_hw2.2.png">
    
6.  Remove the bushing from the old bracket and move it to the new bracket.
    
    <image: "move_bushing.png">
    
7.  Place the new bracket into the robot and verify the Ethernet connector is centered before tightening the mounting screws. Tighten the mounting screws to 2.6 Nm.
    
    <image: "correct_alignment.png">
    
8.  Reassemble the robot by following the steps from [step 3](#Pull) in reverse.
