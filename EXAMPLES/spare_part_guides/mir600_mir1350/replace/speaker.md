## Things you will need

*   A flat-head screwdriver
    
*   A torque wrench ranging from 5 Nm to 11.6 Nm with the following exchangeable sockets and bits
    
*   A long 7 mm socket
        
*   T30
        
*   T45
        
*   T50

## Kit contents

1 × speaker unit with cable

## Before replacing the speaker

1.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position.
    
    <image: "Disconnect_v2.png">
    
2.  Unscrew the six M8 screws and four M10 screws in the top plate. Use a T45 bit for the M8 and a T50 bit for the M10 screws. Tighten all 10 screws to 11.6 Nm when reassembling the robot.
    
    <image: "top_cover_all_screws.png">
    
3.  Remove the top plate from the robot. Use a crane or be at least two people to lift it.
    
    <image: "top_cover_remove.png">
    
4.  Turn the two screws on the front compartment hatch 90˚, and pull out the electronics drawer.
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_1.png"> <image: "access_front_compartment_2.png">
    
5.  Remove the front compartment top cover by unscrewing the six M6×10 screws. Use a T30 bit. When reassembling, tighten these screws to 5 Nm.
    
    <image: "front_compartment_top_screws.png">
    
    <image: "front_copmartment_top_remove.png">

## Replacing the speaker

1.  Locate the speaker in the right-hand side of the electronics drawer cavity.
    
    <image: "600_1350_speaker_placement.png">
    
2.  Trace the speaker wire backwards, and unplug the speaker wire's Molex connector.
    
    <image: "600_1350_DC_molex_connector_speaker.png">
    
3.  Cut the necessary cable ties in order to remove the speaker and wire from the robot.
    
4.  Remove the two nuts holding the speaker, and remove the speaker. Use a deep 7 mm socket.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_speaker_nuts.png">
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_speaker.png">
    
5.  Mount the new speaker unit, and connect it to the male Molex connector in the robot.
    
6.  Secure the new speaker's wire the same way as the original wire using cable ties.
    
7.  Reassemble the robot by following the steps in [Before replacing the speaker](#Before) in reverse.

## Testing

1.  Turn on the robot.
    
2.  Open the robot interface, and go to **Setup > Sounds**.
    
3.  Select a default sound from the list to open **Edit sound**.
    
4.  Verify that **Volume** is set to 100%.
    
5.  Select **Play on robot**.
    
6.  Verify that the sound plays loud and clear with no distortion.

## Troubleshooting

If no sound is coming from the robot check that:

*   The Molex connector for the speaker is properly connected.
    
*   No other wires relating to the speaker have accidentially been disconnected during the replacement.
