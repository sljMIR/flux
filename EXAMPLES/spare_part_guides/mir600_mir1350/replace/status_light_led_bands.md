## Things you will need

*   A socket wrench with the following sockets:
    
*   7 mm
        
*   A pair of small bent nose pliers or a specific wedge lock removal tool for Deutsch connectors
    
*   A pair of wire cutters
    
*   A 4 mm flat-head screwdriver

## Kit contents

1 × front status light LED band

1 × rear status light LED band

2 × side status light LED band

## Before replacing the status light LED bands

1.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position.
    
    <image: "Disconnect_v2.png">

## Replacing the side status light LED bands

1.  Press the two buttons on the side hatch.
    
    <image: "press_hatch_buttons.png">
    
2.  Pull open the hatch.
    
    <image: "open_hatch.png">
    
3.  Disconnect the status light cable.
    
    <image: "disconnect_light.png">
    
4.  Remove the five M4 nuts holding the status light LED band in place. Use a 7 mm socket.
    
    <image: "remove_LED_band_screws_side_hatch.png">
    
5.  Remove the old status light LED band from the side hatch.
    
    <image: "remove_LED_band_600_1350.png">
    
6.  Mount the new status light LED band and reassemble the robot by following [Replacing the side status light LED bands](#Replacin) in reverse.

## Replacing the rear status light LED band

1.  Press the two buttons on the rear hatch.
    
2.  Pull open the hatch.
    
3.  Disconnect the status light cable.
    
4.  Remove the three M4 nuts holding the status light LED band in place. Use a 7 mm socket.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_status_light_LED_nuts.png">
    
5.  Remove the old status LED band from the rear hatch.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_rear_status_light_LED_strip.png">
    
6.  Mount the new status light LED band and reassemble the robot by following [Replacing the rear status light LED band](#Replacin2) in reverse.

## Removing the front status light LED band

1.  Turn the two screws on the front compartment hatch 90˚, and pull out the electronics drawer.
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_1.png"> <image: "access_front_compartment_2.png">
    
2.  Detach the Deutsch connector for the front status light LED band from the bracket. Use a 4 mm flat-head screwdriver to wedge in below the connector and pry it upward while pushing the connector to the right. The screwdriver should be angled so it points toward the electronics drawer front to gain the proper leverage.
    
    <image: "600_1350_pry_deutsch_connector_upwards.png">
    
3.  Disconnect the front status light LED band from the internal connector.
    
    <image: "600_1350_disconnect_LED_plug_front.png">
    
4.  Cut the old male side Deutsch connector for the old front status LED band to feed the wiring through the metal bracket on the back of the electronics drawer front.
    
    <image: "600_1350_cut_old_LED_plug_front.png">
    
    <image: "feed_LED_strip_wire_backward.png">
    
5.  Unscrew the three M4 nuts holding the status light LED band in place. Use a 7 mm socket.
    
    <image: "remove_screws_front_LED_strip.png">
    
6.  Remove the old status light LED band.
    
    <image: "remove_front_LED_strip.png">

## Installing the new front status light LED band

The spare part front status light LED band comes with a Deutsch connector already installed, however, in order to feed the wire through the electronics drawer backplate, the Deutsch connector must be removed and then reattached.

1.  Remove the wedge lock from the Deutsch connector. Use a thin pair of bent nose pliers or a small L-shaped hex key to hook the wedge out. If available, we recommend a specific wedge lock removal tool.
    
    <image: "deutch_connector_remove_wedge_lock.png">
    
2.  Push the release mechanism for the connector pins. Use a small flat-head screwdriver or similar. While pressing the release mechanism, pull the corresponding wire out from the back of the connector.
    
    <image: "press_release_mechnism_deutsch_connector.png">
    
    <image: "remove_wires_deutch_connector_LED.png">
    
3.  Feed the wire through the hole in the backplate.
    
    <image: "feed_wires_through_backplate.png">
    
4.  Reconnect the wires to the Deutsch connector and reinsert the wedge lock.
    
5.  Connect the new front status light LED connector to the internal wiring and refit the plug onto the plastic clip on the electronics drawer backplate.
    
    <image: "remount_LED_plugs_backplate.png">
    
6.  Mount the new front status LED band and close the electronics drawer.

## Testing

Reconnect the battery, and turn on the robot.

1.  Verify that the status light LED band that you replaced is functioning correctly, that the lighting is uniform, and that there are no dead LEDs.
    
2.  Test the various states of the robot and confirm that the status light displays the correct color.

## Troubleshooting

The status light LED band is not emitting any light.

*   Confirm that the Deutsch connector has been assembled correctly, and that the wires are connected to the correct terminals in the connector.
