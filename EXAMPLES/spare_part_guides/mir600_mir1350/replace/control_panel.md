Failure to use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE) can result in serious injury.

Determine relevant and necessary PPE based on the task, environment, and experience of personnel. The following is a list of common PPE you must consider:

*   Safety shoes to protect from impact or puncture hazards.
    
*   Safety glasses and face shield to protect from flying debris or chemical agents.
    
*   Hard hat to protect from falling or flying objects.
    
*   Gloves to protect against sharp objects, electrical hazards, chemical agents, and extreme temperatures.
    
*   Ear plugs or earmuffs to protect from high noise hazards.
    
*   Dust mask or respirator to protect from inhaling chemical agents or other harmful airborne hazards.

Electrical components can be damaged by electrostatic discharge. Consider moving the robot to an ESD protected area and using ESD protected clothing and tools.

## Things you will need

*   A torque wrench ranging from 2.6 to 11.6 Nm with the following exchangeable bits
    
*   T30
        
*   T45
        
*   T50

## Kit contents

1 × Complete control panel

1 × Operating mode key

## Before replacing the control panel

1.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position.
    
    <image: "Disconnect_v2.png">
    
2.  Turn the two hinges in the bottom corners to release the hatch, and remove the hatch from the robot.
    
    <image: "turn_hatch_hinges.png">
    
3.  Press the two buttons on the rear hatch, and pull open the hatch.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_1.png"> <image: "access_rear_compartment_2.png">
    
4.  Disconnect the status light cable.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_3.png">
    
5.  Turn the two hinges in the bottom corners to release the hatch, and remove the hatch from the robot.
    
    <image: "access_rear_compartment_4.png">
    
6.  Remove the rear-left corner cover:
    
1.  Press the Emergency stop button on the corner cover. After reassembling, remember to release the button again.
        
2.  Remove the four screws from the corner cover. Use a T30 bit. When reattaching the corner covers, tighten the screws to 11.6 Nm.
        
        <image: "access_corner_shield_1.png">
        
3.  Disconnect the main proximity board cable harness connector within the side compartment, next to the control panel.
        
        <image: "disconnect_main_proxy_harness_connector.png">
        
4.  Remove the corner cover. It may require some force to disconnect the shield from the corner bumper. Pull it downward firmly to remove it from the bumper.
        
        <image: "corner_remove 1.png">
        
7.  Unscrew the six M8 screws and four M10 screws in the top plate. Use a T45 bit for the M8 and a T50 bit for the M10 screws. Tighten all 10 screws to 11.6 Nm when reassembling the robot.
    
    <image: "top_cover_all_screws.png"> <image: "top_cover_remove_screws_no_covers.png">
    
8.  Remove the top plate from the robot. Use a crane or be at least two people to lift it.
    
    <image: "top_cover_remove.png"> <image: "top_cover_remove_cover_no_covers.png">

## Replacing the control panel

1.  Disconnect the large cable in the back on the control panel.
    
    <image: "disconnect_cable.png">
    
2.  Remove the three M6 × 10 mounting screws. Use a T30 bit.
    
    <image: "mounting_screws.png">
    
3.  Remove the control panel and replace it with the new one.
    
    <image: "replace_control_panel.png">
    
4.  Screw in the two bottom M6 × 10 screws. Tighten the screws to 2.6 Nm. Use a T30 bit.
    
    <image: "screw_in_bottom.png">
    
5.  Screw in the top M6 × 10 screw. Tighten the screw to 11.6 Nm. Use a T30 bit.
    
    <image: "screw_in_side.png">
    
6.  Reconnect the large control panel cable.
    
    <image: "connect_cable.png">
    
7.  Reassemble the robot by following the steps in [Before replacing the control panel](#Before) in reverse.

## Testing

Turn on the robot and test that each button works as expected and you can change between Manual mode and Automatic mode.
