## Things you will need

*   A flat-head screwdriver
    
*   An 11.6 Nm torque wrench with the following exchangeable bits:
    
*   T30
        
*   T45
        
*   T50
        
*   Cable ties

## Kit contents

*   1 × front corner Ethernet port cable

## Before replacing the front corner Ethernet port cable

1.  Turn off the robot, and disconnect the battery in the left side compartment. To disconnect the battery, pull out the Battery lock pin, and pull the Battery lever downwards until the Battery lock pin is in the middle position.
    
    <image: "Disconnect.png">
    
2.  Unscrew the six M8 screws and four M10 screws in the top plate. Use a T45 bit for the M8 and a T50 bit for the M10 screws. Tighten all 10 screws to 11.6 Nm when reassembling the robot.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_top_plate_screws.png">
    
3.  Remove the top plate from the robot. It is recommended to have two persons lifting the top plate.
    
    <image: "600_1350_remove_top_plate.png">
    
4.  Turn the two screws on the front compartment hatch 90˚. Use a flat-head screwdriver.
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_1.png">
    
5.  Remove the front compartment cover by unscrewing the six M6×10 screws. Use a T30 bit. When reassembling, tighten these screws to 5 Nm.  

    <image: "front_compartment_top_screws.png"><image: "front_copmartment_top_remove.png">
    
6.  Pull out the electronics drawer.
    
    <image: "access_front_compartment_2.png">

## Replacing the front corner Ethernet port cable

1.  Disconnect the Ethernet cable from the RJ45 Ethernet port in the front-right corner.
    
    <image: "600_1350_unplug_ethernet_corner.png">
    
2.  Unplug the Ethernet cable from port 2 in the switch.
    
    <image: "600_1350_unplug_ethernet_switch.png">
    
3.  If possible, disentangle the Ethernet cable from the harness and discard it. If not, keep the cable in the harness.
    
4.  Plug in the new Ethernet cable into port 2 on the switch, and route the Ethernet cable along with the harness over to the RJ45 Ethernet port in the front-right corner. Use cable ties to keep the Ethernet cable together with the harness. Include enough slack for the electronics drawer to fully open.
    
    <image: "600_1350_ethernet_cable.png">
    
5.  Reassemble the robot by following the steps in [Before replacing the front corner Ethernet port cable](#Before) in reverse.

## Testing

Turn on the robot, connect to the robot via an Ethernet cable, and verify that no communication errors occur.

## Troubleshooting

If the robot reports communication errors after replacing the Ethernet cable, verify that:

*   The new Ethernet cable is correctly inserted in both ports. You should be able to hear a click when inserting the connector into the RJ45 ports.
    
*   The new Ethernet cable works by testing it with another device.
